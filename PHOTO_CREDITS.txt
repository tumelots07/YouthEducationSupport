REAL PHOTOGRAPHY CREDITS
========================

The website now uses real photographs instead of generated SVG poster artwork for its
page banners, scholarship cards and event poster cards. Images are hosted by Pexels.
Pexels marks the linked photo pages as free to use. Please review the current Pexels
licence/terms before publishing the site publicly.

PHOTO BEHAVIOUR
- Events containing "prayer", "worship" or "faith" use the real prayer/worship photo.
- Events containing "tutor" or "mentor" use a tutoring photo.
- Events containing "career" or "graduat" use a graduation photo.
- Events containing "volunteer" or "community" use a volunteer/tutoring photo.
- Events containing "study", "workshop" or "seminar" use a group learning photo.
- Other events use a general student study photo.

The images are externally hosted, so the browser needs an internet connection to display them.
